Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world, especially in India. It is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams, with 11 players on each team. The main objective is to score more runs than the opposing team.

Cricket has three major formats:
1. Test Cricket: Played over a maximum of five days, with two innings per team.
2. ODI (One Day International): Each team gets 50 overs to score runs.
3. T20 Cricket: Each team gets 20 overs, making it a shorter and faster format.
In cricket, one over consists of six legal deliveries. Players have different roles, including batsmen, bowlers, wicketkeepers, and all-rounders.

Batsmen score runs by running between wickets or hitting boundaries. A four is scored when the ball reaches the boundary after bouncing, while a six is scored when it crosses the boundary without touching the ground.

Bowlers try to dismiss batsmen through methods such as bowled, caught, LBW, and run out.

Some popular cricket tournaments include the ICC Cricket World Cup, T20 World Cup, Asia Cup, and Indian Premier League (IPL).

Cricket requires teamwork, strategy, concentration, physical fitness, and decision-making skills.
